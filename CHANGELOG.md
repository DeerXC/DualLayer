# Changelog

## m2-layers (first stage playable; in progress: layers, ripple, collision and walking accepted; puzzle logic tested, 2026-11-19)

- Made (2026-11-17): a layer mask on every scene object (A = 1, B = 2, both = 3) and the `Tab` key; a theme per layer (background, light colour, light direction, sky colour) with built-in values and optional `[layer_a]` / `[layer_b]` keys in the config; `models.hlsl` and `FrameConstants` carry the sky colours (144 bytes); the title bar and the triangle count follow the layer. The first switch was a colour fade.
- Fixed (2026-11-17): the first build stopped at `std::min` / `std::max` in `Models::Update` (C2589): `windows.h` defines `min` and `max` as macros when `NOMINMAX` is seen too late (the build was `Release | x64` and showed 0 warnings). See docs/CONVENTIONS.md, "windows.h".
- Made (2026-11-17, known-issue pass): a lost device is handled: `Gpu` in `main.cpp` shuts device, triangle and models down and builds them again on a new `Device` (up to 5 times, then a message and exit); the `F9` key (`[debug] device_reset_key = 1`) runs the same path as a test; `NvOptimusEnablement` and `AmdPowerXpressRequestHighPerformance` are exported.
- Checked (2026-11-17): all numbered checks of M0, M1, M2 and K-1 .. K-4 passed on the laptop (RTX 5060, Debug; K-4 is the Release build).
- Made (2026-11-18): the ripple replaces the fade. `Tab` opens a circle from the centre of the screen: both layers are drawn complete into two off-screen pictures (sRGB colour + their own depth), and `shaders/ripple.hlsl` draws one screen-covering triangle that takes the new layer inside the circle and the old one outside, with a glowing ring that bends the picture a little. While no ripple runs, one layer is drawn straight to the back buffer as before. `Tab` during a ripple turns the circle round: no jump. `LayerRipple.h` holds the timing without any Direct3D. `StateGuard` now also puts back the render target, the viewport, the shader resources and the sampler.
- Changed (2026-11-18): themes are no longer mixed: a layer is always drawn with its own theme, and the background colour is the one of the layer on the screen. `[layers] fade_seconds` is replaced by `[ripple]` (`seconds`, `width`, `distortion`, `center_x`, `center_y`).
- Why: the M2 step "two layers" is the show piece of the work: the other world should open up where the player looks, not fade in.
- Checked (2026-11-18): the full build and the quick check Q-1 .. Q-9 passed on the laptop (RTX 5060): ripple, turning it round, resize during a ripple, no debug-layer warning, no leak at exit, Release build.
- Made (2026-11-18, collision per layer): the camera is a ball (radius 0.35 m) that is pushed out of the solid objects of the layer the screen is going to show. New `scene/SceneData.h`: the scene table moved out of `Models.cpp`, so the renderer and the collision read the same data; a `static_assert` stops the build when a sphere or torus is stretched unevenly or a cylinder is not round. New `scene/Collision.h` (header only, no Windows): exact box, sphere, cylinder and torus, in the local space of each object (the inverse of the world matrix of the renderer); the turning shapes use `Models::SpinSeconds()`, the time of the renderer. During a ripple the new layer is already solid (`Models::CollisionLayer()`). `main.cpp`: collision after the camera and the shapes have moved, `[collision] enabled` / `radius`, the `N` key (collide / noclip), `collide` / `noclip` in the title bar. New `tests/test_collision.cpp`.
- Why: the M2 step "layers: drawing and collision" of the plan; the other layer has to be a different place to move in, not only a different picture.
- Checked (2026-11-18): `tests/test_collision.cpp` passes with g++ (4578 checks, `-Wall -Wextra -Wconversion -Wshadow -Wpedantic`): DirectXMath rotation facts, the layer rule, every shape pushed out to exactly one radius, 4000 random points in both layers at many moments of the turning (never inside, measured in a second, independent way), a settled ball is not pushed again, a walk into the cube is stopped in A and free in B, bad input. Three deliberate bugs in the code (wrong turn sign, wrong order of tilt and turn, inverted layer rule) were each caught.
- Made (2026-11-18, checks in one command): `tools/check.ps1` runs the logic tests, the Debug and Release build, a game run that presses `Tab` and `N`, resizes the window during a ripple and reads the title bar and `logs/dual.log`, and the repository safety check; with `-Commit` (and `-Push`) it commits only when everything passed, through `git audit`. `tests/run_tests.ps1` builds and runs `test_collision` and the new `tests/test_ripple.cpp` (the timing of the ripple). The quick check in `docs/BUILD.md` says which checks the script does and which are left for the eye.
- Checked (2026-11-18): `tools/check.ps1` ran on the laptop and everything passed: the logic tests built with MSVC and ran (core, camera, collision, ripple, mesh), `Debug | x64` and `Release | x64` built, the game run (Tab, Tab twice, resize during a ripple, N, a clean close, no `[ERROR]` in the log), and the repository check. The eye checks Q-10 .. Q-13 (collision per layer) were done and passed.
- Changed (2026-11-18): the build notes in `docs/BUILD.md` are written in a neutral record voice (no second person), as `git audit` asks.
- Made (2026-11-19, walking): a body that walks, falls, lands and jumps, with the camera as its eye. New `scene/Player.h` (header only, no Windows): three balls of the collision radius (feet, halfway, eye) pushed out of the solids of the visible layer with `ResolveSphere`; gravity and a jump that rises exactly the jump height; the push direction is the normal of what was hit, so a floor ends a fall and a slope steeper than about 53 degrees turns the fall into a slide; a probe below the feet decides whether the body stands; coyote time (0.08 s) and a jump buffer (0.10 s); a frame is cut into steps shorter than the radius so that the 0.2 m floor cannot be jumped through; a body that falls 20 m below the floor goes back to the start. The layer is a parameter of every update, so the body falls at once when the object it stands on is not in the new layer. `main.cpp`: walking is the default, `F` walks / flies, `Space` jumps, `N` (collision off) also flies, the title bar ends with `collide | walk`, `collide | fly` or `noclip`, the game logs `jump peak` at every landing and `fell off the floor`. Config `[player]`: `walk`, `walk_speed`, `gravity`, `jump_height`, `eye_height`.
- Why: the M2 step toward a playable body. The jump height 2.0 m is chosen to clear the 1.8 m objects, so that a player can stand on them and see the layer switch take the ground away.
- Checked (2026-11-19): `tests/test_player.cpp` passes with g++ (68 checks at the time): standing, falling from a height, walking in four directions, two keys not faster, looking up or down not slower, the jump height measured as the highest point of the feet (0.5, 1.2 and 2.0 m), one landing report, jump buffer, coyote time, the respawn after falling off the floor, the red cube stopping the body in A and not in B, standing on the cube and on the cylinder, a jump onto the cylinder, falling when the layer under the feet disappears, sliding off a 60 degree slope, a random run of 40000 steps in both layers, and bad input. Eleven deliberate bugs in the code were each caught.
- Checked (2026-11-19): `tools/check.ps1` ran on the laptop and everything passed: the logic tests built with MSVC and ran (core, camera, collision, ripple, player, mesh), `Debug | x64` and `Release | x64` built, the game run (walking is the default, `W` walks on the floor, `Space` jumps about the jump height, `F` walks / flies, Tab, Tab twice, resize during a ripple, `N`, a clean close, no `[ERROR]` in the log) and the repository check. The eye checks Q-10, Q-11 and Q-19 .. Q-21 (walking, falling off the floor, standing on the cylinder, the slope of the sphere) were done and passed.
- Decided (2026-11-19, the design of the stage): one room of about 2 to 3 minutes, built from about twelve objects that each serve more than one part of the stage. The only rule stays the layer switch; three puzzle pieces are added, each tied to the two layers: a red plate in A that opens a blue door in B, a purple block that exists in both layers (one push moves it in both), and, later, the shadows of the other layer as a hint. A switch into a place where the body does not fit is refused (the ripple turns back) instead of pushing the body out, which would look like a bug. A gravity that turns over in layer B was tried in a branch and dropped: it is common in other games and moves the design away from the two layers.
- Made (2026-11-19, puzzle logic): new `scene/Puzzle.h` (header only, no Windows): the purple block on a 1 m grid (one cell per 0.25 s after 0.2 s of walking straight at a face from the floor; the next cell must be free in both layers and must not hold the door; a refused push names what was in the way and flashes it for 0.6 s), the plate (pressed by the block, or by the body standing on it while in A) and the door (open while the plate is pressed), `Puzzle::Solids(layer)` (the table that the collision and the body use: the fixed objects, the block, the door while closed), `Puzzle::BodyFits` and `LayerGuard` (Switch / Refuse / Ignore; a refusal turns the ripple back after 0.15 s and keeps the old layer solid). `scene/Collision.h`: `ResolveSphere` takes a table (the short form still uses the scene table). `scene/Player.h`: `SetScene` (the table of solids), `Ball(i)`, `WishDirection()` (the walk direction of the keys, which the puzzle reads to see a push).
- Checked (2026-11-19): `tests/test_puzzle.cpp` (new) passes with g++ (63 checks, `-Wall -Wextra -Wconversion -Wshadow -Wpedantic -Wdouble-promotion`) on a small stage of its own: Init refuses nonsense; pushes move the block exactly one cell in both layers, a tap, a walk at 45 degrees and a walk on top of the block do not; a wall of A stops the block while the body is in B and a wall of B while it is in A, the edge of the allowed cells and the door stop it, and the refusal names the blocker and flashes; the plate and the door in both layers, a closed door stops the body; the guard refuses, holds the old layer, turns back once after 0.15 s and ignores requests meanwhile; and the stage is solved by the block: the high platform of B (2.4 m above the floor) cannot be reached from the floor with any of 84 jump timings, and can be reached from the block once it rests on the plate, the same push that opens the door. `tests/test_player.cpp` passes with 72 checks (four new ones on `WishDirection`). Seventeen deliberate bugs in `Puzzle.h` were each caught, after three tests were added for the two that first survived.
- Checked (2026-11-19): `tools/check.ps1` ran on the laptop with the puzzle logic and everything passed: `Puzzle.h`, `tests/test_puzzle.cpp` and the changed `Collision.h` / `Player.h` built with MSVC, all logic tests ran, `Debug | x64` and `Release | x64` built, the game run and the repository check.
