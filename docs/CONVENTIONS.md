# Conventions

Decided once, used everywhere.

| Item | Rule | Why / check |
|---|---|---|
| Length | metres | |
| Angles | radians in code; degrees in data files (config), converted on load and marked `UNITS` | `[test_triangle] spin_deg_per_sec` |
| Coordinate system | **left-handed, +Y up, +X right, +Z forward** | DirectXMath `...LH` functions |
| Matrices | DirectXMath row-major, **row vectors**: `v' = v * M`; order `World * View * Projection` | `Triangle::Draw` |
| HLSL | `#pragma pack_matrix(row_major)` (`common.hlsli`) **and** the compile flag `D3DCOMPILE_PACK_MATRIX_ROW_MAJOR`; `mul(v, M)`; **no CPU-side transpose** | the M0 test triangle |
| Winding | clockwise = front face (Direct3D default) | the test triangle is clockwise seen from the camera |
| UV | origin top-left (Direct3D). Blender's origin is bottom-left: the exporter flips V | M1 exporter test (asymmetric "F" model) |
| Colour | textures loaded as sRGB; lighting in linear space; the back buffer is written through an sRGB view (`R8G8B8A8_UNORM_SRGB` on a `R8G8B8A8_UNORM` flip-model swap chain) | `Device::CreateBackBufferView`; every colour in `config` is linear |
| Depth | **D32_FLOAT, compare LESS, cleared to 1.0 (far)** (`Device::BeginFrame`, `Models::CreateStates`, and the depth of the two ripple pictures). Reverse-Z was considered and **not adopted** (decided 2026-11-18): the scene is small, and it would change four places. Revisit it only if far objects flicker | the models: a sphere in front of the floor hides it, behind it does not |
| Mesh vertex | position (3 floats) + normal (3 floats) = 24 bytes, no UV yet; indices are 32 bit | `MeshData.h` static_assert, input layout in `Models.cpp` |
| Mesh winding | clockwise seen from OUTSIDE; the outward normal is `cross(v1 - v0, v2 - v0)` (component formula); every shape is centred on its own origin, 1 m big | `tests/test_mesh.cpp`, every triangle of every shape |
| Normals | moved with the inverse transpose of the world matrix, not with the world matrix | `Models::DrawObjects`, `ObjectConstants.normalMatrix` |
| Layers | a layer is a label on an object, not a place in the world: a bit mask, **A = 1, B = 2, both = 3** (`SceneObject.layerMask`); a layer is numbered 0 (A) or 1 (B) in code | `kScene` in `scene/SceneData.h`, the `Tab` key |
| Layer look | each layer has its own theme (background and fog colour, fog density, light colour, light direction, sky colour, the tint and the weathering of the city); a layer is always drawn with ITS theme, themes are never mixed. A is the whole city (warm, clear), B the city after the storm (cold, foggy, mossy). Only the city takes the tint and the weathering: an object of both layers that does not glow (`color.a` = 1 in the model shader) | `[layer_a]`, `[layer_b]` in `config/default.ini`, `DefaultThemes` in `render/Models.cpp` |
| World colours | a thing of one world is drawn in its material: wood or iron for A (made by people, whole), fallen masonry for B (left by the storm); red (A) and blue (B) are only its edge in its own world and its outline in the other, and the colour of the things of a mechanism (orbs, pads, columns of light). An orb takes the colour of the world it is in. A thing works only with what is in its own world: a held orb is placed only on a socket or plate of the layer of the body; machinery (the lock wheel) works only in A | `StageHints::WorldTinted`, `MechanismSet::WorldColour`, `MechanismSet::SpotAhead`, `Canal` |
| Layer pictures | during a ripple each layer is drawn complete into its own picture: colour `R8G8B8A8_UNORM_SRGB` (writing converts linear to sRGB, sampling converts back) and its own `D32_FLOAT` depth, the size of the back buffer; the ripple shader only chooses between the two pictures | `Models::DrawRipple`, `shaders/ripple.hlsl` |
| Scene table | what is in the scene (shape, place, scale, tilt, turn, colour, layer mask) is ONE table, `scene/SceneData.h`, read by the renderer and by the collision. A shape is 1 m big before the scale. World matrix = Scale * RotationX(tilt) * RotationY(spin * time + yaw) * Translation (the yaw: a fixed turn, such as a house turned to its street; the static batch bakes the same). `SceneIsSupported()` stops the build when a sphere / torus is stretched unevenly or a cylinder is not round | `static_assert` in `SceneData.h` |
| Table as a parameter | the collision and the walking body take the table of objects as a parameter (`ResolveSphere(..., objects, count)`, `Player::SetScene`); the short forms use the scene table of the program. The puzzle builds its table every frame (`Puzzle::Solids`), because its block moves and its door opens | `scene/Collision.h`, `scene/Player.h`, `scene/Puzzle.h` |
| Collision body | the flying camera is a ball (centre = the camera, radius `[collision] radius`, default 0.35 m); it is pushed out of the solids, never the other way. A solid exists only in the layers of its mask; during a ripple the layer that is opening counts (`Models::CollisionLayer`) | `scene/Collision.h`, `tests/test_collision.cpp` |
| Walking body | three balls of the collision radius at the feet, halfway and at the eye (`[player] eye_height`, default 1.55 m); the camera is the eye; the feet are the point on the ground. Walking is the default, `F` flies, collision off also flies | `scene/Player.h`, `tests/test_player.cpp` |
| Walking numbers | `[player]` gravity 30 m/s^2, jump height 2.0 m (reaches the top of the 1.8 m objects), walk speed 5.5 m/s, fall speed at most 40 m/s; coyote time 0.08 s, jump buffer 0.10 s; the ground counts as standable when its push points up by at least 0.6 of its length (about 53 degrees) | constants at the top of `Player.h`, `[player]` in `config/default.ini` |
| Walking steps | a frame is cut into steps that move the body by less than 0.7 of the radius, and a frame longer than 0.1 s counts as 0.1 s, so that nothing is jumped over (the floor is 0.2 m thick) | `Player::Update` |
| Out of reach | a surface that a body must not reach is at least 2.3 m over every floor in reach of it (a jump is 2.0 m); a parapet round a high floor is 2.6 m. A jump that ends higher in another room is a skip | `tests/test_reach.cpp`, `Stage::kParapet` |
| Glow | `SceneObject::glow`: a light of its own, added as albedo * glow in the model shader (`params.w`), the same in both layers; 0 for ordinary objects, about 0.3 for hints, about 1 for lamps, orbs and columns of light | `shaders/models.hlsl`, `render/Models.cpp` |
| Overlay | screen pixels, (0, 0) at the top left, +y down; colours sRGB with alpha, converted to linear when drawn; drawn last, no depth | `render/OverlayBatch.h`, `render/Overlay.cpp` |
| Static batch | the fixed city is baked into world-space vertices (`BatchVertex`, 56 bytes: position, normal, linear colour with the city flag in alpha, the shader parameters); only cubes that do not turn are baked; the first `Stage::StaticDrawCount` entries of the drawn table are the baked ones | `render/StaticBatch.h`, `tests/test_batch.cpp` |
| Stairs and slopes | stairs are walked as the ramp under them and drawn as steps of at most 0.3 m (the body snaps down 0.35 m); a slope steeper than about 53 degrees is not stood on (roofs); a railing (1.1 m) stops a walk, a parapet (2.6 m) seals a floor | `scene/Shapes.h`, `tests/test_shapes.cpp` |
| Water | layer A only, fed by the aqueduct (broken in B); never solid for the body (feet 0.3 m under the surface: back to the lamp or entry of the room), solid for orbs (they float); the canal has a level that a wheel raises | `Shapes::Water`, `Stage::Submerged`, `Stage::kWaterFeeds`, `Canal::Submerged` |
| Fall line | per room: 6 m below the bottom of the room (`Stage::FallLine`); the lowest point of an orb is `Stage::kFallY` | `TestFallLines` |
| Moving things | exist in both layers; only where they are may differ (the crate of the canal floats in A, lies on the bed in B); moved in one, they move in both (the drift) | `Canal::Crate` |
| Prism | belongs to no world: worked in either layer, drawn the same in both, white; what it moves changes world, it does not come and go | `TriggerKind::Prism`, `Gate::swapsLayer` |
| Switch guard | a switch is refused when the body would overlap anything solid of the target layer (the whole table), or be under its water | `Puzzle::FitsIn`, `Recovery::SwitchAllowed` |
| Places of return | a fall or R comes back to the lit lamp of the room, else its entry; under the water of the layer of the body, to the entry of the dry room next to it | `Recovery::SafeRespawn`, `TestRecovery` |
| Ground | a height field of two triangles per cell, split from the (x0, z0) corner to the (x1, z1) corner; `Height()` is the height of those triangles (drawn and solid alike); flat-shaded; solid for the body in both layers. The town is levelled 5 cm under the street floors (`kPlainY`); under every place of the map without a floor at street level the ground is a pit at -16 (`kPits`), below every fall line | `scene/Terrain.h`, `scene/Landscape.h`, `tests/test_terrain.cpp` |
| Sea | one box per layer at -9 (`kSeaY`), round the eye in steps of 20 m, 720 m across: wider than the far plane; not solid | `Landscape::AddSea` |
| Far plane | 300 m (`render/Projection.h`); the land and the sea are sized by `Landscape::kViewDistance`, which must not be smaller | `static_assert` in `main.cpp` |
| near, far | never a name in code that `windows.h` sees: it defines `near` and `far` as empty macros | `scene/Landscape.h` |
| Orbs | never stuck: an orb at rest that no body of its world can take back goes to its start | `scene/OrbReturn.h` |
| Puzzle grid | the crate of the courtyard (the block) lives on a 1 m grid, the flagstones of the yard: cell (x, z) has its centre at world (x, z), on the floor. A stage says which cells the block may use (`StageDesc::minCell` .. `maxCell`) | `scene/Puzzle.h` |
| Crate (block) | wood with iron bands, the wood of the crate of the canal; the only thing that is solid in BOTH layers and can move. One move = one cell in 0.25 s, after 0.2 s of walking straight at a face (within about 37 degrees) from the floor. The next cell must be free in both layers and must not hold the door; a refused push names what was in the way (`LastBlocker`) and it flashes for 0.6 s, because it may be in the layer that is not on the screen | `Puzzle::Update`, `tests/test_puzzle.cpp` |
| Plate and door | the plate (a brass slab, gold: a thing to use) is in layer A and is not solid; it is pressed by the block resting on it, or by the body standing on it while the body is in A. The door (fallen masonry) is in layer B, solid while closed, open while the plate is pressed. Every question about them takes the layer the body is in (a body that switches to B leaves the plate) | `Puzzle::PlatePressed`, `Puzzle::DoorOpen` |
| Switch guard | a layer switch into a place where the body does not fit is never pushed through: the ripple opens for 0.15 s and turns back, and the old layer stays solid meanwhile (`LayerGuard::Refuse`). A blocked request during a ripple is dropped. The push-out of the collision stays only as a safety net | `Puzzle::BodyFits`, `LayerGuard`, `tests/test_puzzle.cpp` |
| Rotation on a row vector | DirectXMath `RotationY(a)`: x' = x cos a + z sin a, z' = -x sin a + z cos a (+90 degrees turns +Z into +X). `RotationX(a)`: y' = y cos a - z sin a, z' = y sin a + z cos a (+90 degrees turns +Y into +Z). The collision writes these out; the test pins them | `Collision.h` `RotateX` / `RotateY` |
| Right of a direction | in this left-handed space the right of a flat forward direction f is (f.z, 0, -f.x): forward +Z gives right +X | `Player::Update`, `tests/test_player.cpp` (strafing) |
| Ripple units | the circle is measured in screen HEIGHTS (so it is round on a wide screen); the centre is 0 .. 1 over the screen, (0, 0) = top left | `[ripple]` in `config/default.ini`, `FarthestCornerDistance` |
| windows.h | `min` and `max` are macros unless `NOMINMAX` is defined **before the first include that pulls in `windows.h`** (also through other headers). Where this order is not certain, do not use `std::min` / `std::max`; `std::clamp` and a plain `?:` are safe | `Models::Update` (the first M2 build failed with C2589), `LayerRipple.h`, `Collision.h`, `Player.h`, `Puzzle.h` |
| Feature level | 11_0 minimum (11_1 used when present) | |
| File encoding | UTF-8, no BOM, LF line ends (`.gitattributes`, `.editorconfig`) | |
| Naming | types and functions `PascalCase`, variables `camelCase`, members `m_`, constants `kPascalCase`, `enum class` | `.clang-format` formats, it does not rename |
